Delete your account?

Deleting your account withdraws your consent under our privacy policy.

  • You will be signed out and can no longer use this account.
  • Your cards that nobody has ordered are taken off sale.
  • Your account and its activity are removed 30 days after your last order ends.
  • You cannot delete it while an order you bought or sold is still pending or shipped.
  • This cannot be undone.
